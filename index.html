What PSI(T) is

The Physics Society of IISER Thiruvananthapuram has been an open organization under the guidance of the School of Physics, with the primary objective of promoting physics among students across all levels.

It aims to serve as a friendly platform that promotes conceptually intriguing, thought-provoking discussion — the kind that makes coursework click and curiosity easier to follow through on.
